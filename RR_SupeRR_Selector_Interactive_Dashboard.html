How the model works

01 · RECOGNIZE

Pre-ball pressure

Late overs, wickets lost, collapses, required-rate stress, tight chases and knockout stakes. The highest 25% within format and innings are classed as pressure.

02 · ADJUST

Context + baseline

Player contributions are adjusted to same-format, same-season, same-innings and same-phase peers, then compared with their own ordinary performance.

03 · TRUST

Sample + uncertainty

Minimum IPL evidence, empirical-Bayes shrinkage, positive-match consistency, clustered uncertainty and 400 match-level bootstrap resamples.
